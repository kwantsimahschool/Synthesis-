About Unclench

We started in 2024 with one question: why is the easiest stress tool also the one nobody carries?

Company information

Name: Unclench Ltd.
Founded: 2024
Team: 12 people and a very squished office dog
Products: stress balls, putty, fidget rings, scent sachets, weighted pads

Every product is designed by people who have been through exam season, shift work or burnout themselves.

Our values

Calm over hype. Honest claims: our products help you relax, they do not treat medical conditions. Fair pay for everyone who makes them.

Meet the founder

Kwantsimah Orleans Boham, founder and CEO

Boham studied computer science and spent three years working in a university counselling office before starting Unclench.

She noticed students were handed leaflets about stress but rarely something to do with their hands in the moment. Her first prototype was a sock filled with rice and lavender. It sold out at a campus fair in an afternoon.

Today she leads product design and speaks at schools about small, practical ways to cope.
